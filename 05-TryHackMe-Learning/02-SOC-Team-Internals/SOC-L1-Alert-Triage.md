## Overview
Completed a practical SOC Level 1 simulation focused on alert prioritisation, investigation, verdict classification and incident escalation using a simulated SIEM dashboard.

## Key Concepts
- **Security Events:** Activities recorded by operating systems, networks and applications.
- **SIEM & EDR:** Platforms used to collect security telemetry, detect suspicious activity and generate alerts.
- **Alert Prioritisation:** Assessing severity, timestamps and investigation status to determine response order.
- **Alert Investigation:** Reviewing affected users, hosts, IP addresses, event details and surrounding activity.
- **Verdict Classification:** Determining whether an alert is a True Positive or False Positive.
- **Escalation:** Referring confirmed or complex security incidents to L2 analysts.

### L1 Triage Workflow
1. Prioritise and select an unassigned alert.
2. Assign the alert and change its status to `In Progress`.
3. Review alert details and relevant indicators.
4. Follow the applicable investigation workbook or playbook.
5. Investigate related activity in SIEM logs.
6. Determine the alert verdict and document findings.
7. Escalate to L2 when necessary or close the alert.
